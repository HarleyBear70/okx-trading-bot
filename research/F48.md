## Rules (written before testing)
- **N (crypto long):** day k: close > SMA100, 6-day vol / 100-day vol < 0.5, day k range is the narrowest of last 4 days (NR4). Enter at close of k+1 if it closes above day k's high. Exit close below lowest low of prior 5 days or 15 bars. Stop 3 ATR(20).
- **S (forex both ways):** 3 straight higher closes -> short, 3 lower -> long. Exit at first close the other way or 5 bars. Stop 3 ATR.
- **P (forex both ways):** close above SMA200 and lowest close of 5 days -> long (mirror for short). Exit close back across SMA5 or 10 bars. Stop 3 ATR.
