## What it means
- **Trading the edges of a range** wins about half the time on daily charts, but the moves inside a 30-day range are small next to fees, so it nets almost nothing. On never-seen coins it made 0% since 2022.
- **Breakout + retest** is the best version of this advice: profit factor 4.86 and a −4.4% worst drop. But clean ranges with a clean retest are rare (37 trades in 8 years, about 4 a year), so it makes about 2-3% a year. Its trades are a subset of Donchian's breakouts, which already catches them (without waiting) plus the many breakouts that never come back, which is where most of the profit is.
- Not added as a bot. "Control risk" is already built into every bot (fixed % risk, ATR stops, 10% stop rule).
