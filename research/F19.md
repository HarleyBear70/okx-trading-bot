## What it means
- **Limit-order entries** are the closest to a real improvement: on Trend Rider's coins they make +165% vs +145% with a smaller drop, because the fee is lower and buying a small dip after the break gives a better price. But 28% of signals never fill (48% on more volatile new coins), and the missed ones include runaway breakouts, so on new coins it was worse in 2018-21. It could be tried later as a live demo experiment with the new execution log measuring real fill rates.
- **More coins** add profit only by adding risk; at the same drop it's the same bot.
- **Slower candles** cut fees and drops but miss too much since 2022.
- Trend Rider stays as it is. With 19 rounds of tests, it is close to as good as this rule family gets.
