## What it means
- Most popular trend scripts (Ichimoku, Keltner, Hull, Vortex, SAR) work, but on daily crypto they take almost the same trades as Donchian (correlation 0.86-0.96): adding one would double the bet on the same trends, not add a new edge.
- Golden cross, EMA cross and the original Follow Line hold too long and give back big gains (drops of 12-40%).
- Mean reversion (triple RSI, like RSI(2) in F4) wins small and fees eat it.
- The one strong different system is #5, a variant of Follow Line where the trend line resets on the first day the close is above the upper Bollinger band (so it flips up sooner than the original). It passes every rule, including never-seen coins and correlation 0.75 with Donchian. Because it came out of 14 tests and is not the published script, it should go to paper (forward test) first, not straight to demo.
