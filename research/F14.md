## What it means
- **Candlestick patterns** (engulfing, hammer, morning star) have no lasting edge on their own: each made money in 2018-21 and lost or broke even since 2022 on new coins. The engulfing result is mostly "a green day in an uptrend", which Donchian already trades.
- **Chart-pattern breakouts work**, like every other breakout test today. The double bottom is the cleanest: it wins about half the time with small drops, and it held up on new coins (+148% / +47% there). It is rare (about 9 trades a year on 5 coins), so it fits as a small, high-quality extra rather than a main bot.
- The ascending triangle is good on the main coins but weaker on new ones.
