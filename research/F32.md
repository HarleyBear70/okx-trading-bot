Verdict: **promising but not proven.** The 52-week-high factor with the BTC switch beat the BTC protected hold since
2022 (+224% vs +136%) with a similar worst drop (-41% vs -39%), and the switch is what makes every factor survive
(off: drops of 77-99%). Short-term reversal fails. BUT the universe was picked with hindsight (SOL, AVAX, DOGE are
coins we now know became big; LUNA and FTT were added to soften that), so 2018-21's +4,880% is inflated.
These are hold-pile-sized drops (40%+), not a 0.5%-risk trading bot. Next step before any paper bot: rerun on a
point-in-time top-10 universe (as N2 did) and with top 2/5 to check it isn't luck.
