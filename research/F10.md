## What it means
- **Pyramiding works best on Donchian.** Adding a second piece once a breakout is up 1R (with the stop then at least at the first entry) puts more money on the trends that keep going. At 0.32% risk it makes +152% vs +123% with the same drop, better in both halves and on never-seen coins.
- On Trend Rider, pyramiding doubles profit at full risk but also the drop; scaled down to fit the 10% rule it only adds 9 points, and it was mixed on new coins.
- A wider trail (4 ATR) on Trend Rider looked good on its own coins but made almost nothing on new coins since 2022. Tighter (2.5 ATR) is worse. Trend Rider keeps 3 ATR.
- Volatility sizing is already built in (ATR stops + fixed % risk).
