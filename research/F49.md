**Data limits (same as F45-F48):** FXCM, Yahoo, Stooq, ECB, Frankfurter, Binance Vision are blocked. Crypto = Binance.US daily (works). Forex = Fed H.10 daily noon rates (github datasets/exchange-rates): closes only, no highs/lows, no DXY, no 4H. Stops checked on closes, so forex numbers are indicative. Script `f49_scout.py`, output `F49_output.txt` (its first 16 lines "D"/"G" are F46 re-running on import, ignore them). Halves: crypto split 2022-01-01; forex 2012-18 / 2019-now.

## Sources

## Rules (written before testing)
- **H (crypto long):** close >= 95% of highest close of last 252 days, close > SMA200, fresh (yesterday was not already within 5%). Exit close < 85% of the 252-day high. Stop 3 ATR(20).
- **X (forex cross):** ratio GBP/USD / EUR/USD (= synthetic EUR/GBP). z-score of log ratio over 60 days. z < -2 long, z > 2 short. Exit z crosses 0 or 20 bars. Stop 3 ATR. Cost 0.02% a side (two legs).
- **V (forex value):** z of log price vs 500-day mean. z < -1.5 long, > 1.5 short. Exit z crosses 0 or 60 bars. Stop 10 ATR. Each pair alone.
- 0.5% risk, 10x leverage cap. Crypto fees 0.35% and 0.02%; seen BTC ETH SOL XRP BNB LINK, never-seen ADA DOGE AVAX LTC DOT.

## Verdict

Caveat: it is a trend/breakout cousin of G (F46), Donchian and Turtle - buys strength near a yearly high - so it probably overlaps them; overlap with Trend Rider/Donchian NOT tested.
