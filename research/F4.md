## What it means
- **Volume confirmation:** it turns SP-12 v4 from a loser into a small winner, because it halves the trades and therefore the fees. But it only makes about 4% a year, with PF 1.19 since 2022, and it does nothing for Donchian. v4 stays retired; the bots already running are better.
- **RSI(2) "buy the dip" fails:** it wins 66% of the time, but the wins are too small to pay 0.7% a round trip.
- **RSI(14) momentum has a real edge** (works in both halves and on never-seen coins), but its exit (RSI back under 40) is slow, so in 2021 it gave back a big part of its open profit, and the drop stays above 10% even at 0.2% risk. It overlaps Donchian (correlation 0.75). A faster exit would be its own test.
- **"Smart money" (copying OKX's top traders):** not testable yet. The OKX connection expired during this run, and the signal history only goes back 90 days, too short for the 8-year standard. It could only be judged by a paper forward test.
