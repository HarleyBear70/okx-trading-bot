## What it means
- **Anchored VWAP** almost never blocks anything: when a coin breaks out to a new high, it is nearly always above the average price since the last swing low. It's a good chart-reading tool, but it adds nothing to a breakout bot.
- **Overbought (RSI > 80)** blocked only 3 of 675 Trend Rider buys; breakouts rarely start that stretched, and the few it skipped were a mix of winners and losers.
- **OBV confirmation** cuts about a quarter of the trades: it helped in 2018-21 but hurt since 2022 (Trend Rider) or the other way round (Donchian), and it didn't help on new coins. Mixed, not added.
- None of the three is adopted; the bots stay as they are.
