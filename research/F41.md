## Verdict
- **Learning which strategy to fund doesn't beat simply holding the strongest strategy.** On crypto the learners make more than equal weight (+165% to +179% vs +144%) but with bigger drops, so return per drop is about the same (~17); all of them trail Turtle 55/20 alone (+303% / -13.6%, ~22).
- **On forex a learner can't create an edge that isn't there:** every manager is flat or negative because 3 of the 4 members lose since 2019.
- Takeaway for building: the strongest forward-test candidates are the members themselves: Turtle 55/20 (crypto) and RSI(2) (both markets, small but steady). They go to paper forward testing on Binance.US.
