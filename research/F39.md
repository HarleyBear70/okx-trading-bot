## Verdict
- **RSI(2) 5/95 is the first rule that makes money on both markets:** forex PF 1.35 (win 68%, worst drop -1.3%), crypto PF 1.73 (win 70%, drop -2.0%). Forex 2012-18 was flat (PF 1.00); 2019-now PF 1.67 / win 73%, which is when forex trend following failed (F38), so the two complement each other.
- **But the edge per trade is small and trades are rare** (~11 a year on forex, ~17 on crypto), so at 0.5% risk it makes only a few % over the whole period. It's a high-win-rate, low-stress setup, not a money machine. RSI(2) 10/90 is flat on forex.
- **London-open (Asian range) breakout fails badly:** -85%, 6,634 trades, PF 0.76.
- Use: a simple manual setup for Jeff on EUR/USD + GBP/USD (and crypto), or a small Binance.US paper bot; not a replacement for the trend bots.
