# F47: research scout 2026-11-07 - 2 forex ideas tested (close-only data), 0 passed

**Data:** FXCM/Yahoo/Stooq/FRED/ECB/Binance Vision/Bitfinex/Kraken are all blocked from this sandbox. Only GitHub raw works, so forex uses daily Fed H.10 noon rates (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datasets/exchange-rates/main/data/daily.csv, saved to /tmp/fx.csv, inverted to EUR/USD and GBP/USD). **No highs/lows, no 4H, no DXY**: stops are checked on closes only and "ATR" is the 20-day mean close change. Numbers are indicative, not as good as the FXCM tests. Crypto was not re-tested (F45/F46 already cover the daily crypto engine). Script `f47_fx_close.py`, output `F47_output.txt`.

## Sources
- Month-end rebalancing: Melvin & Prins ECB FX workshop 2013 (equity gains -> USD buying before the fix); HSBC's Brent Donnelly says the trade "appears to have been arbitraged away": https://www.poundsterlinglive.com/eurusd/11424-month-end-is-dead-hsbc-s-donnelly ; also https://www.fxempire.com/education/article/fx-seasonality-what-still-works-and-what-doesnt-in-2026-1545003 . No rigorous cost-adjusted backtest found.
- Bollinger fade: classic quant thesis (see F43); from F39, mean reversion is the only family that worked on forex.

## Rules (fixed before testing; same as F43)
- M: close of 3rd-from-last trading day of the month, SHORT EUR/USD and GBP/USD; exit close of 1st trading day of next month; stop 3 ATR. Mirror (long) also run.
- B: close below SMA20-2sd -> long, above SMA20+2sd -> short; exit first close back across SMA20; stop 3 ATR.
- 0.01% a side, 0.5% risk, 10x leverage cap, halves 2012-03..2018 / 2019..now.

| B Bollinger fade | GBP/USD | +3.4%, PF 1.13 | +4.0%, PF 1.13 |

## Verdict
- M: fail. Short loses or is flat in both halves; the mirror flips sign between halves. No stable month-end edge (matches the HSBC comment).
- B: fail. PF 0.83-1.16, below 1.3; EUR/USD lost money in the first half. Small positive lean, same "small edge" story as RSI(2) in F39.
- Nothing passed. Nothing wired into bot.py.
