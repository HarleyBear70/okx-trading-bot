## Rules (fixed before testing; script f45_scout.py)
- M turn of month: long at close of the 3rd-from-last trading day, exit close of 1st day of next month, stop 3 ATR(20).
- B Bollinger stretch fade: long when close < SMA20 - 2sd, exit first close above SMA20, stop 3 ATR(20).
- S squeeze breakout: Bollinger bandwidth at its 120-day low (within last 5 days) and close above upper band -> long, exit first close below SMA20, stop = 10-day low.
- One coin at a time, $1000, 0.5% risk, fee 0.35% and 0.02% a side. Seen: BTC ETH SOL XRP BNB LINK. Never-seen: ADA DOGE AVAX LTC DOT.
