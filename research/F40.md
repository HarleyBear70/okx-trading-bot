## Verdict: all three session strategies fail.
- The average move per session candle is only 0-3 pips, about the size of the spread, and it changes sign between periods: there is no reliable "London goes up / New York goes down" pattern to trade.
- S1 London breakout with the trend: -60% (3,095 trades). Adding the trend filter helped vs F39's plain breakout (-85%) but not enough.
- S2 New York fade: -58%.
- S3 dollar trend + 4H BOS both ways with a trailing stop: about break-even (PF 0.98).
- On EUR/USD + GBP/USD the only tested edge so far is RSI(2) 5/95 mean reversion (F39), plus the DXY filter for manual trading (FOREX_DXY.md).
