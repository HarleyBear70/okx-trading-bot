Findings:
- Both scripts work on the daily chart and both lose to fees on 4H. The 0.04% / 0.05% commission in the scripts' TradingView settings hides this; set it to 0.35% in TradingView.
- Trendline Break does better without its fixed 2R take profit (+31% vs +12%), matching every earlier test: fixed targets cut the big crypto winners.
- A3 (Trendline Break, trail only) is the most useful: it passes every rule and is only weakly correlated with Donchian (0.44), so it trades different moves. On its own it makes far less than Donchian.
- B1 (Confluence Rank) passes but overlaps Donchian (0.74). Loosened to 75 it becomes almost a Donchian copy (0.91).
- Next step if Jeff agrees: run A3 as a paper bot (no money), then check at the Oct 19 review whether adding it to the line-up improves combined return/DD.
