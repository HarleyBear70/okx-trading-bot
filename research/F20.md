## What it means
- The script's core (a channel breakout with ATR stops) is exactly Donchian Trend, which is already live and better: it lets winners run instead of taking a fixed 2 ATR profit (the same lesson as F1, F12 and the 24 fixed-target tests).
- Adaptive lookback and the losing-streak size cut don't add anything; like F8 and F16, adjusting after losses mostly makes the bot smaller right before the next big win.
- The article's own disclaimer ("I've been liquidated multiple times", futures with leverage) is a good reason to keep the bots on spot, without leverage.
