# F8 results: bots that learn without hurting themselves

Plan: research/F8_PLAN.md. Script: research/f8_learn.py. Run 2026-10-06. Real fee 0.35% a side.

## L1 drawdown brake (half size while the balance is X% below its best)

| Bot | No brake (2018-now / 1st half / 2nd half) | Brake at −5% | Brake at −8% |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trend Rider | +134% / −9.6% · +49% · +57% | +101% / −9.8% · +43% · +39% | identical to no brake |
| Donchian Trend | +123% / −9.1% · +63% · +37% | +119% / −8.7% · +63% · +34% | identical to no brake |
| NNFX | +45% / −7.6% · +20% · +21% | identical | identical |

At −8% the brake never switches on. The bots' closed-trade balances rarely fall that far, because the stops already keep losses small. At −5% it switches on during normal losing streaks, so the bot is at half size when the recovery comes: Trend Rider loses a quarter of its profit with no smaller drop. **Fail.** The existing rule (stop a bot that falls 10% from its best) stays the safety net.

## L2 AI trade filter (walk-forward logistic regression on Trend Rider's entries)

| Test years | Unfiltered | Skip the model's bottom 30% | Skip the bottom 50% |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020-now | **+123% / −10.3% / PF 1.72** (595 trades) | +80% / −8.3% / 1.74 (441) | +56% / −9.3% / 1.68 (335) |
| 2020-21 | +37% / −8.9% | +32% / −8.3% | +34% / −6.3% (PF 2.35) |
| 2022-now | +63% / −10.3% | +36% / −8.3% | +17% / −9.1% |

The model, trained only on earlier trades (yearly, no look-ahead), skips about as many big winners as losers. Profit factor barely moves, so skipping trades mostly costs profit. **Fail.** Trend Rider's wins come from a few big trends that look like ordinary entries at the start; nothing known at entry picks them out.

## Conclusion
No automatic learning beat the plain bots. The bots keep learning the safe way: every trade is journaled, the watcher and weekly Scout compare live results with the backtest, and any change is tested on 8 years (both halves, never-seen coins, real fees) before it goes live.
