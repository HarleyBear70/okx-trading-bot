## What it means
- **The bots already obey the 3-5-7 rule.** With 0.32-0.5% risk per trade, stops moving to breakeven at +1R, and trailing stops, the combined open risk never got to 5% in 8 years, so a 5% cap would never fire.
- **Limiting bots per coin** lowers the drop a little (−6.8% vs −7.4%) but cuts profit more (+94% vs +109%): the days several bots buy the same coin are the strong trend days. Lower return/DD in both halves, so not added.
- **Running the three bots together is the real safety net:** the combined worst drop is −7.4%, smaller than any single bot's (−9.6% to −10%), because they win and lose at different times.
