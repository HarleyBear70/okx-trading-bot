## What it means
- **Waiting for a discount pullback hurts.** The strongest crypto breakouts often never pull back to 50%, so the bot misses them and is left with the weaker ones that come back (and often keep falling). Win rate drops from 37% to 29%.
- **Requiring a liquidity sweep picks better trades** (win rate 41%, smaller drops) but skips too many good ones, so it makes less than half the profit.
- Trend Rider's plain BOS entry stays. Smart-money structure (the BOS itself) is what works here; the extra entry filters don't add profit after fees.
- The SP-12 bots, built from 12 smart-money checks (order blocks, FVGs, sweeps, BOS), found the same thing: those checks counted against a buy (research/RESEARCH.md), and the bots lost to fees.
