Verdict: the fee is the biggest lever found so far. At the same risk, Binance.US fees give more profit AND smaller
drops (0.75%: +608% / -12.1% vs +366% / -14.7%; since 2022 +155% vs +84%). Jeff has a Binance.US account (it won't
link to TradingView, which the bots don't need). Moving the bots needs a Binance.US API connection in bot.py, paper
testing on Binance.US prices first (no Binance.US demo account), then small live. Strategies that failed only on
fees (e.g. SP-12 v4: +124% at 0.1% vs -2% at 0.35%) should be re-tested at 0.02%.
