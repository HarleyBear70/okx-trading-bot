# F18 results: entries and position sizing

Plan: research/F18_PLAN.md (F18b added before its run). Scripts: research/f18_sizing.py, research/f10_exits.py. Run 2026-10-06. Real fee 0.35% a side. Donchian here is the plain version (no pyramid) at 0.32% risk, as in F10.

## Averaging down (add an equal piece halfway to the stop)
| Bot | Single entry (2018-now; 1st · 2nd half return/DD) | Averaging down | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trend Rider | +145% / −10.3% (5.7 · 6.1) | +155% / −12.2% (5.3 · 5.2) | no: worse return/DD in both halves, bigger drops |
| Donchian | +109% / −8.4% (9.5 · 4.0) | +115% / −9.2% (10.2 · 3.4) | no: worse since 2022 |
Adding to a loser doubles the loss whenever the stop is hit, and most trades that fall halfway to the stop keep going.

## Sizing (single entry), scaled to similar drops (F18b)
| Bot | Risk-based (live) | Fixed $ per trade | Fixed % of balance per trade |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trend Rider | **+145% / −10.3%** | $100: +104% / −12.5% (2nd half −14.8%) | 6%: +81% / −12.6% |
| Donchian (plain) | +109% / −8.4% | $80: +197% / −11.1% (+140% · +57%) | 6%: +263% / −12.2% (+149% · +46%) |
At the first, larger sizes (25%/20% of the balance, $250/$200 per trade) the drops were 19-44%, so those runs aren't comparable (F18 raw: TR fixed % +766% / −43.8%, DC fixed % +2830% / −34.7%).

## What it means
- **Trend Rider: keep risk-based sizing.** Its stops vary a lot (1-3 ATR under a swing low), and sizing by the stop keeps every loss the same size; fixed sizes make it worse on every measure.
- **Donchian: fixed sizing looks better** (about +200-260% vs +109% at a slightly bigger drop). Its stops are always 2 ATR, so risk-based sizing gives very small positions on the most volatile breakouts, which are often the big winners. It went just over 10% drop and wasn't checked on never-seen coins or with pyramiding (live), so it is a lead, not a change: next test = Donchian live (pyramid + leash) with fixed-% sizing at a size that keeps the drop under 10%, on own and never-seen coins.
- **Averaging down: no.**
