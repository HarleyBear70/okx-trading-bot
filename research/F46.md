**Forex was not tested.** FXCM, Yahoo, Stooq, FRED, ECB and Frankfurter are all blocked (HTTP 403) from this sandbox, so there is no EUR/USD, GBP/USD or DXY history. Crypto daily candles come from the Binance.US API (works). Halves split 2022-01-01. Same simulator, coins, fees and risk (0.5%) as F45 (script f46_scout.py, output F46_output.txt). F43's forex rules (month-end dollar flow, Bollinger fade) are still waiting.

Sources: web search found no rigorous daily-bar paper; closest is https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11708 (crypto trend following with out-of-sample costs; suggests trend beats mean reversion at medium speed) and the Han/Kang/Ryu cost-adjusted momentum study (time-series momentum holds after costs, cross-sectional does not). Ideas below are my own rule variants, not copied.

## Rules (fixed before testing)
- D dip in uptrend: close > SMA100, 3 lower closes in a row, 3-day drop >= 8% -> long. Exit first close above prior day's high, or after 10 bars. Stop 3 ATR(20).
- G gated fast breakout: close > SMA200 and SMA50 > SMA200, close = highest close of 10 days (fresh signal) -> long. Exit close below lowest close of prior 5 days. Stop 3 ATR(20).
- Seen: BTC ETH SOL XRP BNB LINK. Never-seen: ADA DOGE AVAX LTC DOT.

## Results at 0.35% fee (avg per coin; PF = avg of per-coin PF)
| Idea | 1st half | 2nd half | Never-seen 1st / 2nd | Trades |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D dip | -0.1%, PF 1.00 | +0.3%, PF high (few trades) | +0.9% / +0.3% | 31 / 77 seen |
| G gated breakout | **+4.2%, PF 2.9** | **+3.7%, PF 2.1** | +3.6% / +1.1% (PF 2.3 / 1.9) | 99 / 195 seen |

At 0.02% fee: G +4.6% / +4.6% seen, PF 3.2 / 2.5. D barely changes (+0.0% / +0.5%).
Worst drop for G: 2.9% (small only because risk is 0.5% a trade). Coins up in the 2nd half: seen 5/6, never-seen 2/5.

## Verdict
- D: fail. First half is flat (PF 1.00 seen), few trades, tiny gains.
- G: passes the rule (both halves positive, PF >= 1.3, drop small, positive on never-seen coins). **Candidate only.** Caveats: it is a faster cousin of Donchian/Turtle (10-day breakout with 200-day gate), so it likely overlaps them; I did NOT test whether it adds to Trend Rider/Donchian. Never-seen 2nd half is weak (2 of 5 coins up). 4x more trades than Donchian means fees matter more. Next step: overlap test against Donchian 20 and Turtle 55/20 before anything else.
