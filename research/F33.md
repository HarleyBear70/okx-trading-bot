Verdict: **context only, not a bot rule.**
- Asset managers' positioning flipped meaning between the halves (high readings were bullish in 2018-21, bearish since 2022), so it isn't reliable.
- Leveraged funds (hedge funds) work as a contrarian sign in both halves: when they are most short vs their past year, BTC's next 4 weeks were better (+12.4% vs +7.1% average in 2018-21; +3.7% vs +2.2% since 2022). Shorts are fuel for squeezes. But the edge is small, weekly data gives few samples, and since 2024 much of their short is ETF basis hedging rather than a bearish bet.
- Use: a line of context in the News Watch / Analyst brief ("hedge funds heavily short = squeeze fuel"), not a trade trigger.
