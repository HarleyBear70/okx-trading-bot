Verdict: **neither passes.**
- V (vol sizing) makes more money, but mostly because it trades bigger on average: worst drops rise past the 10% limit (-11.4% / -11.7%). Return per unit of drop barely moves (13.9 -> 14.5, 16.0 -> 16.2), so at equal risk it's about the same bot. Not worth the complexity.
- R (relative strength) cuts 30-40% of trades and loses a third of the profit on both bots, with lower return per drop. The breakouts it skips include many winners: alts often break out *before* they lead BTC on a 30-day view.
