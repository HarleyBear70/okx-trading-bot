--- TIMEFRAMES (Ep 11) ---
1. Always top-down: Daily -> 4H -> 15m. Never build the bias from the 15m.
2. Only look for an entry on the 15m after price TOUCHES a 4H zone. Until then, do nothing.

--- DAILY BIAS (Ep 15) ---
3. On the Daily/4H: draw the swing range of the last break of structure + premium/discount.
   Mark yesterday's high/low and any equal highs/lows (liquidity).
   Premium = sells only. Discount = buys only. Middle = no trade.
4. The bias is wrong if a candle BODY closes beyond the key level (not a wick).
   If that happens, reset the bias. No revenge trades, no doubling down.

--- MARKET STRUCTURE (Ep 5) ---
5. Break of structure / market shift = a candle BODY closes through the level. A wick does NOT count
   (a wick through = possible liquidity grab).
6. Each break of structure sets the new swing range: swing low = lowest point that led to the break,
   swing high = highest point before the pullback starts.
7. Market shift (trend flip) = body close through the STRONG high/low (Must Hold).
   Internal shift = body close through the internal high/low = pullback warning only, not a trend flip.

--- ZONES (Ep 7) ---
8. A zone is the ORIGIN of a big, strong move (big candles / imbalance).
   Method = OPEN (Range: high-to-low of the consolidation or pullback before the move,
   OR Pivot: the single turning candle incl. its wick).
9. A zone is "used up" (mitigated) once price comes back to its 50%. A touch that doesn't reach 50%
   leaves it unmitigated. A used-up zone usually fails next time.
10. Only use zones that match the trend (demand in an uptrend) and sit on the right side of the 50%.
    Prefer the extreme zone nearest the Must Hold. Ignore zones on the wrong side.

--- LIQUIDITY (Ep 13) ---
11. Liquidity sits above swing highs / equal highs and below swing lows / equal lows, trend lines,
    obvious support/resistance.
12. For a long, price must SWEEP liquidity below a recent low (wick through) and snap back in a
    V-shape. A sweep alone is not the entry - still need the market shift.
    "No liquidity sweep, no entry."

--- ENTRY MODEL (Ep 18) - CONSERVATIVE / WAY A ---
13. Sequence for a long:
    a) price reaches a valid 4H demand zone in discount
    b) liquidity sweep below a recent low (V-shape)
    c) market shift on the 15m: candle BODY closes above the last lower high
    d) wait for price to pull back into the small zone that created the shift
    e) confirmation at that zone: rejection (wick/doji) then a strong candle my way -> enter
    No pullback after the shift = no Way A trade (Way B may apply, rule 36).
14. Stop: just below the market-shift candle.
15. Target: one target only - top of the drop candle / next opposing zone / next swing high.
16. One try per market shift. If it fails (price breaks the low), wait for a brand-new setup.
17. Minimum risk-reward: OPEN (Brad uses 2). Do not filter on this until Jeff decides.

--- A+ CHECKLIST (Ep 25) ---
18. 1) direction matches the smaller AND bigger trend  2) good zone (matches trend, swept liquidity)
    3) price is INSIDE the zone  4) sweep + market shift  5) killzone (OPEN)  6) risk-reward (OPEN)
    A+ = all pass. A = meets the plan but not every box (OK). B/C = never trade.
    Risk per trade: OPEN (currently 0.5% on demo). Never size up to win back losses.

--- FROM BRAD'S LIVE BREAKDOWNS ---
25. Sweep or shift? Even after a body close through the strong low/high, wait for follow-through:
    - V-shape snap back + body close back through the last lower high = LIQUIDITY SWEEP (trend continues)
    - Price keeps going and makes a new internal break of structure = the MARKET SHIFT is valid.
    Until one of these happens: no clear bias = stay out.
26. Rank zones: A = extreme zone at the last swing high/low (strongest, esp. if it swept liquidity),
    then B, then C (closest, weakest). Take profit at the nearest opposing zone first.
27. The entry pattern must show up on the 15m. The 4H does not need its own market shift.
28. Reversal = all 5: bias + zones; wait for the zone; candle signs at the zone (signs only);
    liquidity sweep; market shift (= confirmation).
29. Stop a little beyond the sweep's high/low so a second sweep doesn't take me out.
30. Reversals often start right before or at the London / New York open. Have zones marked before.
31. Map structure layer by layer (swing -> 1st internal -> 2nd internal). Don't mix layers.
33. A wick through a low/high with no body close and no follow-through = be skeptical of a shift;
    under equal lows/highs it's probably a liquidity sweep.
34. Take profit at the NEXT level price is most likely to reach first. No far "final boss" targets.

--- JEFF'S OWN RULES ---
35. Market shift entry timing (Jeff): goes in around the 2nd candle after the shift is confirmed
    when using Way B; holds patiently and stays in unless the market shifts back.
36. WAY B (backup, when there is NO pullback): enter around the 2nd candle after the shift.
    Stop halfway down or at the bottom of the big candle that pushed the shift.
    (Brad calls entering without the pullback the aggressive version.)
37. Trailing: once price moves my way, trail the stop to just below the MS candle,
    then under each new higher low (mirror for shorts). Don't trail too tight.
