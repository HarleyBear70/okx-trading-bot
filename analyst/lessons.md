# The Analyst's lessons journal

Read at the start of every session. A lesson is a habit, not a rule change: anything that would change a bot's rules
needs a backtest (both halves, never-seen coins, 0.35% fee) and Jeff's OK.

## Standing lessons (from the research, before the first trade)
1. Fees are 0.7% a round trip. Trades need room to make 3%+ or they aren't worth it. (FEES.md, D1_DAY.md)
2. Big winners pay for everything. In Trend Rider's backtest, a few long trends made most of the profit.
   Don't cut winners short with tight targets. (F30: fixed 1R/2R targets failed)
3. Don't buy right under resistance on fading volume. Wait for the breakout, or for a pullback to support.
4. The bots already buy the obvious breakouts. My value is judgment: sitting out bad markets, reading news and
   event risk, and cutting trades that stop making sense before the stop is hit.
5. Self-tuning after recent results failed every test (F8). Change habits slowly, and only on evidence from many trades.

## Jeff's chart-reading playbook (Smart Money Concepts), use in every session
Read in this order, higher timeframe first: daily, then 4H, then 1H only to time an entry.
1. **Market structure (MS):** swings, higher highs/lows or lower highs/lows; the latest BOS and any change of character.
   Bullish or bearish? HTF candles and structure outweigh LTF.
2. **Points of interest (POI):** order blocks, supply/demand zones, flip zones (old resistance turned support and the
   reverse), fair value gaps / imbalances (inefficiency that price tends to come back and fill, making it efficient).
3. **Premium vs discount** of the current swing range: buy in discount, be careful buying in premium.
4. **Liquidity:** equal highs/lows and obvious stops sitting above/below; inducement (a minor high/low that lures early
   traders in before the real move); sweeps of that liquidity before reversals.
5. **Candles, only at a POI with MS behind them:** body vs wick (a big body = momentum and pressure; long wicks =
   rejection), the larger the candle the more momentum, dojis = indecision. Never trade a candle pattern alone.
Caution from the research (F3, SP-12): as rigid rules, the 50% discount wait and full SMC checklists cut out the biggest
breakouts and lost to fees. Use the playbook to judge whether a setup is real and where to place entries and stops,
not as a reason to skip every strong breakout that never pulls back.
