# P/L calendar

Updated automatically every hour (2026-10-05 09:37 EDT). Days are Eastern time. Green = closed profit that day, red = closed loss, grey = no closed trades; stronger colour = bigger day (full colour at 2% of the balance). Each square: the day's % change of the balance and the $ amount. DEMO = OKX demo account orders, PAPER = simulated, no orders. Made by pl_calendar.py.
